# Business research adversary

Gather what the outside world actually thinks. This is a context step,
required for serious work, and it runs before discussion and spec.

## Rules

- Research only. Drafting belongs to later stages.
- Find adversarial, specific, recent context that matters to the
  stakeholders. Skip the generic web summary.
- Look for strong opinions, objections, buyer anxieties, competitor praise and
  complaints, expert disagreement, and the language real people use.
- Keep internal facts separate from external discourse.
- For legal, compliance, payroll, tax, HR, insurance, security, or regulated
  work, use authoritative sources only.

## Read

- `.business/context/context.md`
- `.business/context/known-facts.md`

## Write

- `.business/context/research-adversary.md`, with these sections:
  - topic or decision being tested
  - stakeholder groups researched
  - niche objections
  - buyer anxieties
  - category language
  - competitor praise and complaints
  - recent discourse and timing shifts
  - expert disagreement
  - naive-sounding claims to avoid
  - implications for the business spec
