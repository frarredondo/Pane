# Intent and searcher job

Classify intent before choosing structure.

## Intent classes

| Intent | Searcher job | Page obligation |
|---|---|---|
| Informational | Understand a concept or process | Answer clearly and early, outside any product frame |
| How-to | Complete a task | Give steps, examples, tools, pitfalls, and outcome checks |
| Template | Get a usable artifact | Put the template or example near the top, with little preamble |
| Comparison | Choose between options | Define criteria, compare fairly, disclose tradeoffs |
| Alternatives | Replace a known option | Include real alternatives in the expected category |
| Commercial category | Shortlist tools/vendors | Provide criteria, category map, use cases, and honest fit |
| Troubleshooting | Fix a problem | Lead with likely causes and fixes, then background |
| Brand/query navigation | Reach or understand a known brand | Help the reader reach the brand; leave out unrelated product copy |

## Expected answer set

For listicles, alternatives, and comparisons, decide what belongs in the answer
set before deciding what the brand wants to promote.

- "ChatGPT alternatives" expects AI chatbots or tools used in place of ChatGPT.
- "Project management software" expects tools in that category, not generic
  productivity essays.
- "Template" queries expect downloadable or copyable artifacts, not a sales
  page with a buried template.

A product outside the expected answer set must not be ranked or framed as part
of it.

## Intent fit checklist

- The primary intent is stated in one sentence.
- Secondary intents are listed and stay secondary.
- The page type matches the job.
- The outline reaches the answer before the pitch.
- The product role follows product fit, not ownership of the site.
