# Self-promotion fairness

Use this for alternatives pages, comparisons, listicles, category pages, and
product-led informational content.

## Same-rules test

- Is the brand evaluated with the same criteria as competitors?
- Is the brand section similar in length and structure?
- Are the brand's limitations specific and useful?
- Are competitor limitations fair, sourced, or based on firsthand review?
- Would the order still make sense with the names hidden?
- Does the brand belong in the expected answer set?

## Structural red flags

- The brand is ranked first without evidence.
- The brand section is 2x or more longer than competitors'.
- Competitor cons are specific while brand cons are vague.
- The list includes irrelevant tools to justify the brand's inclusion.
- CTAs appear before criteria or useful options.
- Internal links route mostly to more self-promotional listicles.

## Acceptable promotion

Promotion is acceptable when:

- The product is a direct or contextual fit.
- Criteria are clear before the pitch.
- The reader gets a useful recommendation without clicking.
- The CTA is a next step after the answer.
