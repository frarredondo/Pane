# Site audit workflow

Use this workflow to reach an evidenced explanation for traffic loss or content
decay.

## Audit sequence

1. Define the scope: whole site, section, template family, competitor, or URL
   set.
2. Build the inventory: sitemap, crawl, URL list, or export.
3. Segment the inventory by section, template, topic, intent, page type, and
   product fit.
4. Merge performance data if available.
5. Separate demand loss from ranking loss.
6. Map important lost queries to current SERP winners.
7. Sample pages from winners and losers.
8. Identify recurring fingerprints:
   - answer distortion
   - CTA overload
   - schema issues
   - stale names
   - list padding
   - repeated headings
   - unfair comparison patterns
9. Rank hypotheses by evidence strength.
10. Turn the findings into action buckets and a recovery sequence.

## Evidence strength

Strong:
- URL-level ranking loss with a current SERP replacement.
- A repeated issue across many pages in one section or template.
- The lost query still has demand and competitors still rank.
- Page samples show a clear intent mismatch or template footprint.

Medium:
- The traffic drop lines up with a Google update.
- A section declined more than the rest of the domain.
- Similar pages share promotional or technical patterns.

Weak:
- Backlink changes without page or query evidence.
- General claims that AI overviews killed traffic.
- Topical overreach claims when core-topic pages also declined.

## Causality rule

Treat Google update timing as correlation until URL, query, or SERP evidence
supports it.
