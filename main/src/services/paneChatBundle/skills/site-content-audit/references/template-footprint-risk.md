# Template footprint risk

One mediocre page is a page problem. Thousands of structurally similar mediocre
pages can become a section or site quality problem.

## Risk signals

- Repeated intros that restate the keyword.
- Identical H2 and H3 skeletons across many pages.
- The same CTA blocks in the same positions.
- The same product walkthrough embedded in unrelated topics.
- An own-product section much longer than competitor sections.
- Vague limitations for the own product and specific criticism for competitors.
- Broken dynamic year variables in schema or metadata.
- List padding to justify product placement.
- Generic pros and cons that could apply to any tool.
- Internal links that route to more promotional listicles over useful next
  steps.

## Section-level questions

- Does this template help the reader, or only scale keyword capture?
- Would a human editor build the same structure for each query?
- Does the product role change with intent, or is it always forced?
- Are weak pages being pruned, or is more of the same being published?

## Severity

Low:
- The pattern appears on a few pages, and intent still matches.

Medium:
- The pattern repeats across a section and creates generic pages.

High:
- The pattern repeats across hundreds or thousands of URLs and distorts answer
  sets or the product role.

Critical:
- The same weak pattern expanded during a decline when it should have been
  pruned or rewritten.
